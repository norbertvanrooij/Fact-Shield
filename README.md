# Fact Shield privacy policy

Effective date: 2026-05-11

Fact Shield uses an external time-service Action only to retrieve the current local time needed for its response header.

## What data may be sent
When the Action is used, Fact Shield may send a time zone value, such as `Europe/Berlin`, to an external time service provider to retrieve the current local time.

## What data is not intentionally sent
Fact Shield is not designed to send your full conversation content, name, email address, account credentials, or other direct personal identifiers to the time-service provider unless you include that information yourself in a prompt.

## Storage and sharing
I do not intentionally store the timezone data returned by the time-service Action beyond what is needed to generate the response. I do not sell personal data. An external time-service provider may temporarily process or log requests under its own operational and privacy practices.

## Purpose of processing
The sole purpose of this Action is to fetch the current time for the relevant time zone so that the GPT can display an accurate time header in its replies.

## Third-party services
Fact Shield may rely on an external time-service provider to return current time data. That provider may process request metadata in accordance with its own terms and privacy practices.

## Your choices
You can choose not to use this GPT if you do not want a time zone value sent to an external time service provider.
